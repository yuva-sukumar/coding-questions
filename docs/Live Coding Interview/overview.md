# What Happens in a Coding Interview?

Live coding interviews are one of the most misunderstood stages of technical hiring.

Many candidates practice hundreds of coding problems but still struggle during real interviews.  
The reason is simple:

> Coding interviews evaluate **how you think, communicate, and solve problems**, not just whether your code works.

This guide explains what actually happens during a live coding round and what interviewers expect from candidates at each step.

---

## Why This Guide Exists

Most preparation focuses only on solving problems online.  
However, real interviews are interactive and assess additional skills such as:

- problem understanding
- structured thinking
- communication
- debugging ability
- collaboration mindset

Understanding this process reduces anxiety and helps you prepare effectively.
